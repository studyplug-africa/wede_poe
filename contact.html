Frequently asked questions

Before you get involved.

Is Bright Futures SA already registered?

This website is a concept for a proposed organisation. Registration and relevant compliance should be completed and verified before making claims about official status.

Can a school request support?

Yes, the proposed process is to invite enquiries from schools and review needs, available resources and partnership arrangements before confirming any assistance.

How will donations be used?

The intention is to use donations for clearly described educational projects and keep records of funds and supplies. A transparent donation and reporting process should be in place before fundraising begins.
